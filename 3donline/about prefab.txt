	<h3>About</h3>
	<p>This is a basic multiplayer game, built in Unity 3D. To play, either put in a room name to join a lobby, or make one yourself. Be sure to set a username before joining to something you want, as that auto made one is often not desired.<br></p>
	<h3>How to play</h3>
	<p>Once you join a lobby, you can use your mouse to look around, and WASD or arrow keys to move. Use space to jump, and hold shift to sprint. Be sure to fullscreen the game with the blue fullscreen button on the bottom left of the game window for the best experience. You can press ESC at anytime while playing to un-fullscreen the game. If you press it while you aren't fullscreened, the game will open the pause menu. Resume closes the pause menu, Disconnect returns you to the main menu, and Quit Game stops the game.<br>
		<b>How to play/use each minigame</b><br>
		<b>Soccer - </b> Push or shove the ball into the opposing net. You run faster here, so use it to your advantage to launch the ball. Preview looks like a bouncing ball in the hub.<br>
		<b>Cosmetics Station - </b> Walk next to a dummy to equip its hat. Looks like a row of fake players in the hub.<br>
		<b>Parkour Tower - </b> A parkour tower, currently features 1 section. Use the glowing orbs to guide you to the next platform, and try to reach the top as fast as possible! You jump higher here. Looks like a bunch of platforms in the hub<br>
		<b>Tag - </b> A large tower style arena for playing tag. Left click to tag someone, you know you are tagged/if someone else is tagged if they are red. Jump in the flowing column in the center to lift yourself upwards to dodge the person who is it. You run fast and jump higher here. Looks like a bunch of flowery platforms in the hub<br>
	</p>
