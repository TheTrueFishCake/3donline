	<h3>About</h3>
	<p>This is a basic multiplayer game, built in Unity 3D. To play, either put in a room name to join a lobby, or make one yourself. Be sure to set a username before joining to something you want, as that auto made one is often not desired.<br></p>
	<h3>How to play</h3>
	<p>Once you join a lobby, you can use your mouse to look around, and WASD or arrow keys to move. Use space to jump, and hold shift to sprint. Be sure to fullscreen the game with the blue fullscreen button on the bottom left of the game window for the best experience. You can press ESC at anytime while playing to un-fullscreen the game. If you press it while you aren't fullscreened, the game will open the pause menu. Resume closes the pause menu, Disconnect returns you to the main menu, and Quit Game stops the game</p>
